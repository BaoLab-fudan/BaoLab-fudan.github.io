Research
AI methods for biological discovery and medicine

We develop AI to connect molecular, cellular, tissue, and clinical data.

AI Methods
Self-supervised Learning
Learning useful biological representations from omics and imaging data with limited labels.
Multimodal Integration
Connecting omics, images, and phenotypes while preserving shared and distinct biological information.
Continuous Space and Time Modeling
Modeling biological variation across continuous spatial coordinates and time to reconstruct tissue organization and dynamic processes.
AI + Biomedicine
Disease Phenotype Reconstruction
Reconstructing disease phenotypes across tissue, cell, and molecular scales from noisy or incomplete measurements.
Target Discovery
Linking pathology, spatial omics, and clinical phenotypes to identify molecular targets.
Drug Discovery
Learning from genetic, perturbation, and imaging data to predict compound function and support drug screening and repurposing.
